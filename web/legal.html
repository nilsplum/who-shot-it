Privacy Policy

Who Shot It? is a party game app. It works without an account, ads, tracking, analytics or cookies. We process only what a game needs, mostly for a few minutes, and keep nothing once the game is over.

Who is responsible

The controller is HiveDone UG (haftungsbeschränkt), see the Imprint. Questions about your data: support@hive-done.com.

On your phone

  • Your photos: with your permission, the app reads your photo library on your phone to pick random photos for you. This happens only on your phone. Nothing is sent before you tap “Yes”; then only the photos on screen are sent, made smaller and without location or other photo data.
  • Saved on your phone: your name, avatar and game settings, which version of these terms you accepted, and the game you are in with a random key for that game. This is strictly necessary for the game you asked for (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). Deleting the app deletes it.

On our server, and why

  • Game data: the name and avatar you choose, a random key for the game, your answers, points and reactions. We need them to run your game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • The photos you confirm: stored only to show them to the players in your game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not look at, analyse or use them for anything else. If other people can be seen in a photo, we process it on the basis of our and your legitimate interest in playing a private game with friends, limited to that game and to the time described below (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Removed players: the random game key of a player the host removed is kept with the game, so they cannot join it again (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Connection data: to deliver the game and protect it from misuse, our host processes your IP address and technical details (such as device type and time) (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not store logs ourselves.
  • Reports and emails: we use them to handle your request or report (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR with Art. 16 DSA, otherwise Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

How long we keep it

Each photo is deleted right after its round, and earlier when it is reported, when its owner changes their choice, leaves or is removed. A game and everything in it is deleted when its last player leaves, and at the latest 24 hours after its last activity. Emails are deleted once your request is handled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Who receives it

  • The players in your game see your name, avatar, points, reactions and the photos you confirm.
  • Our host, Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), runs the server for us under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Games and their photos are stored and processed in data centres in the EU. Access from outside the EU (for example for maintenance or security) cannot be ruled out; for such transfers, Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR), and standard contractual clauses apply in addition. More: Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • The app stores: Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) process data when you download and update the app, as controllers in their own right and under their own privacy policies. We may see anonymous download statistics from them.

We use no other services, no third-party code that collects data, and no automated decision-making.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15 to 20 GDPR). Just email us. Because there are no accounts and games are deleted so quickly, we can often no longer find data about you; please tell us your game code and roughly when you played.

Right to object: you can object at any time, for reasons arising from your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR).

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work (Art. 77 GDPR).

You do not have to give us any data, but without a name you cannot join a game. The game is not meant for anyone under 16.

Report content

In the app: tap ⚑ on any photo. It is deleted for everyone at once.

To us: our single point of contact for users, authorities, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (Art. 11 and 12 DSA) is support@hive-done.com, in English or German. To report illegal content (Art. 16 DSA), email us: the game code and roughly when you saw it, what the content shows, why you believe it is illegal, your name and email (not needed when reporting child sexual abuse material), and a statement that your report is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge.

We handle reports quickly and carefully, confirm them, delete illegal content, and tell you and, where we can reach them, the person who shared it what we decided and why. If content suggests a threat to someone’s life or safety, we inform the authorities. Photos are deleted after their round and games within a day at the latest, so reported content is often gone already.
