Privacy Policy

We keep this short because we collect very little: no account, no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.

Who is responsible

The controller is HiveDone UG (haftungsbeschränkt), see the Imprint. Questions about your data: support@hive-done.com.

What we process and why

  • Game data: the name and avatar you choose, your answers and reactions. We need them to run your game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The other players in your game see them.
  • Your photos: they stay on your device until you tap “Yes”. Then only the photos you see are made smaller, uploaded and shown to the players in your game. Each photo is deleted right after its round, when you change your choice or leave, when the game ends, and at the latest when the game room closes. We do not look at, analyse or use your photos for anything else (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • “All photos” on iPhone: the Apple Shortcut you run sends up to 30 random photos to your private storage in the game room. Only your own device gets them, and we delete them as soon as it has; if it never does, after 15 minutes.
  • Technical data: when you use the site, your browser sends your IP address and technical details (such as browser type and time) to our host. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not store logs ourselves.
  • Email: if you write to us, we use your message only to answer it (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

How long we keep it

A game room and everything in it (names, answers, any photo not yet deleted) is deleted when the last player leaves, and at the latest 2 hours after the last activity in it.

Your device

The game stores a few things in your browser’s local storage, only for the game you asked for: a random player key, your name and avatar, the game you are in, your game settings and whether the iPhone shortcut worked. This is strictly necessary for the service (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is needed. You can delete it in your browser settings at any time.

Report content

Our single point of contact for users, authorities, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (Art. 11 and 12 DSA) is support@hive-done.com, in English or German.

To report illegal content (Art. 16 DSA), email us: the game code and roughly when you saw it, what the content shows, why you believe it is illegal, your name and email (not needed when reporting child sexual abuse material), and a statement that your report is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge.

We handle reports quickly and carefully, delete illegal content, and tell you and, where possible, the person who shared it what we decided and why. Photos are deleted after each round and every game room after at most 2 hours, so reported content is often gone already.
